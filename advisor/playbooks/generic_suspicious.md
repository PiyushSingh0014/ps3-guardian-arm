# Suspicious message
severity: medium
summary: The message shows warning signs of a scam, even though it does not match one known pattern exactly.
pretends: someone you might trust
fact: Scammers use urgency, links and requests for personal details to rush people into mistakes before they can check.

## Steps
1. Do not tap links, call numbers or install anything from this message.
2. Never share an OTP, PIN, CVV or password.
3. If it claims to be from a company or bank, contact them yourself through their official app or website.
4. If money has left your account, call 1930 immediately and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
5. Report the message on Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu) at sancharsaathi.gov.in.
