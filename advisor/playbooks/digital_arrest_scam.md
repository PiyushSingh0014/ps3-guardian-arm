# "Digital arrest" or fake police threat
severity: critical
summary: Someone claims to be from the police, CBI, customs or a court and threatens arrest unless you pay or stay on a video call. Real officials never arrest you or demand money over a call.

## Steps
1. Hang up. Do not stay on video calls, and do not transfer any money.
2. Tell a family member or friend immediately; scammers rely on keeping you isolated and scared.
3. If you are worried about a real case, contact your local police station yourself, using a number you look up.
4. If you already paid, call 1930 immediately and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
5. Report the call or message on Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu) at sancharsaathi.gov.in.
