# Promotional spam
severity: low
summary: This looks like unwanted marketing rather than a scam. It is annoying but usually not dangerous.

## Steps
1. Ignore it; replying can confirm that your number is active.
2. Do not tap links in unexpected offers; open the brand's official app instead.
3. Turn on Do Not Disturb for promotional messages through your mobile operator's app or by contacting 1909.
