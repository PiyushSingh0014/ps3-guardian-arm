# Fake electricity or bill disconnection
severity: high
summary: The message threatens to cut your electricity or service tonight unless you call a number or pay through a link.
pretends: your electricity company
fact: Electricity companies send disconnection notices with the official bill and do not ask you to call a personal mobile number or install an app.

## Steps
1. Do not call the number or pay through the link.
2. Check your bill status in your electricity provider's official app or website, or at its office.
3. Never install apps or "remote support" tools that a caller asks for.
4. If money has left your account, call 1930 immediately and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
5. Report the message on Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu) at sancharsaathi.gov.in.
