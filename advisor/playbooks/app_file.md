# App or program sent as a file
severity: critical
summary: The file is an app or a program, not a document or a photo. Once installed, such apps read your SMS and OTPs and empty bank accounts; fake wedding invitations, challans and KYC forms are sent this way.
pretends: a wedding invitation, a bill or a document from someone you know
wants: to make you install an app that reads your SMS and OTPs and takes over your phone
fact: Real invitations, bills and notices come as photos, PDFs or links to official websites, never as app files; apps should come only from the Play Store.

## Steps
1. Do not open or install the file, and delete it from the chat.
2. If you installed it, switch off mobile data and Wi-Fi, then uninstall the app.
3. Open Settings and remove any app you don't recognise from Accessibility and device-admin access, then run a Google Play Protect scan.
4. Call the person it came from: their WhatsApp may be hacked and sending the file to all their contacts.
5. If money has left your account, call 1930 immediately and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
