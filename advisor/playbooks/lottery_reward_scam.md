# Prize, lottery or refund scam
severity: high
summary: The message promises a prize, cashback or refund but needs a fee, your bank details or an OTP first. Genuine prizes and refunds never ask for these.
pretends: a company or office offering a prize or refund
wants: to steal a fee, your bank details or an OTP with a fake prize or refund
fact: Genuine prizes and refunds never ask for a fee, bank details or an OTP first; income-tax refunds go straight to your bank account.

## Steps
1. Do not pay any "processing", "tax" or "registration" fee, and do not share bank details or OTPs.
2. Do not tap the link. Income-tax refunds go straight to your pre-validated bank account; you never claim them through a link.
3. If you already paid or shared details, contact your bank through its official app or the number on your card.
4. If money has left your account, call 1930 immediately and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
5. Report the message on Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu) at sancharsaathi.gov.in.
