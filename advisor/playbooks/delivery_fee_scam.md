# Fake parcel or delivery fee
severity: medium
summary: The message says a parcel is stuck or needs a small fee. The link leads to a fake page that steals card details.
pretends: a courier company
fact: Courier companies and India Post do not ask for redelivery, address-update or customs fees through links in SMS messages.

## Steps
1. Do not pay through the link, however small the amount.
2. Check the parcel yourself in the courier's official app or website, opened by you, using your real tracking number.
3. If you entered card details, block the card through your bank's official app or the number on the card.
4. If money has left your account, call 1930 immediately and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
5. Report the message on Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu) at sancharsaathi.gov.in.
