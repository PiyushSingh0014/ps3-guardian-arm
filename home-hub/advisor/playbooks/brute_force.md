# Password guessing
severity: high
summary: A device is trying to log in again and again in a short time. Someone is guessing passwords.
pretends: a device on your Wi-Fi
wants: to guess a password and log in to your device or account
fact: People log in a few times at most; dozens of quick login attempts a minute come from password-guessing tools.

## Steps
1. If the target is your device, make sure it uses a long, unique password, and turn off password login where you can (use keys or one-time codes).
2. Disconnect the device doing the guessing if it is on your Wi-Fi.
3. Change the passwords of any account on the targeted service.
