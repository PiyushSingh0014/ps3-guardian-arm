# Fake bank KYC (Know Your Customer) message
severity: high
summary: Someone is pretending to be your bank and saying your account will be blocked unless you "update KYC" through their link or number.
pretends: your bank
wants: to steal your net-banking login, card details or OTP through a fake KYC page
fact: Banks update KYC only at a branch or through their official app or website, and genuine bank links end in .bank.in.

## Steps
1. Do not tap the link or call any number given in the message.
2. Never share your OTP, PIN, CVV or password. Banks never ask for these by SMS, call or link.
3. If you already entered details, open your bank's official app or call the number printed on the back of your card, and block net banking and your cards.
4. If money has left your account, call 1930 immediately and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
5. Report the message on Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu) at sancharsaathi.gov.in, then delete it.
