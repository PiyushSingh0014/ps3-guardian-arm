# Fake website link (phishing)
severity: high
summary: The link leads to a website that is likely fake: it is made to look like a real company or office so that you type your password, card details or OTP into it.
pretends: a bank, shop or service you use
wants: to make you type your password, card details or OTP into a fake website
fact: Real Indian bank websites end in .bank.in and government websites end in .gov.in; a website that only has a company's name in its address, or sits on a free hosting service, is not that company's site.

## Steps
1. Do not open the link. If you opened it, close the page without typing anything.
2. If you typed a password, PIN or card number there, change that password at once from the official app, and call your bank to block the card.
3. To check your account or an offer, open the official app or type the website address yourself.
4. If money has left your account, call 1930 immediately and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
5. Report the message on Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu) at sancharsaathi.gov.in.
