# Document that leads to a fake website
severity: critical
summary: The document leads to a fake website, or is itself a fake sign-in page. Whatever you type there goes to the scammer.
pretends: a bank, a company or a government office
wants: to make you open a fake page and type your password, card details or OTP
fact: Real banks and offices do not send documents that ask you to sign in through a link; their official websites are reached through their app or by typing the address yourself.

## Steps
1. Do not click the links in the document, and do not type anything into it.
2. If you typed a password, PIN or card number, change it at once in the official app or website, and call your bank to block the card.
3. Never share an OTP, whoever asks for it.
4. Delete the file and block the sender.
5. If money has left your account, call 1930 immediately and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
