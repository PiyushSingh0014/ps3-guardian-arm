# Device opened a phishing website
severity: high
summary: A device on your network looked up a website that is on the public list of phishing sites, or that copies a bank or brand. Someone may be about to type a password, card number or OTP into a fake page.
pretends: a bank, shop or service you use
wants: to steal a password, card details or OTP through a fake website
fact: Real Indian bank websites end in .bank.in and government websites end in .gov.in; a site on the phishing lists has been reported for stealing details.

## Steps
1. On that device, close the website without typing anything.
2. If a password, PIN, OTP or card number was typed there, change that password at once from the official app and call your bank to block the card.
3. Find the message or app that opened the link and delete it.
4. If money has left your account, call 1930 immediately and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in.
